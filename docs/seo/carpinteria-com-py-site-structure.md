# carpinteria.com.py — Page & SEO Structure (v2 — short URLs)

**Scope rule:** wood, muebles a medida, carpintería de aluminio/blindex only.
No masonry, pools, chapa roofing, metal structures — those live on obra.com.py.

**URL rule:** short slugs, max 2 path levels, every segment fits Google Ads display path (≤15 chars).

**Conventions:** WhatsApp-first (+595 995 628862 stage 1), no prices, PYG only,
Asunción + Gran Asunción coverage language, AI imagery (no names, no fake team claims).

---

## Page template (every service page)

1. H1: primary keyword + "en Paraguay" / "en Asunción"
2. 3–5 AI images (Paraguayan context)
3. "Cómo trabajamos": foto y medidas por WhatsApp → presupuesto → fabricación e instalación
4. Materials section (melamina, MDF, lapacho, cedro, granito/cuarzo) — material terms carry SEO weight
5. FAQ block, 3–5 questions, FAQ schema (plazos, zonas, garantía, materiales)
6. WhatsApp CTA: hero, mid-page, sticky footer button

## SEO notes

- Primary geo modifier: "asuncion"; check bare + geo. PY volumes are low — head terms carry the site.
- Internal cross-links: /pergolas/ /decks/ /machimbre/ → obra.com.py; /cocinas/ → arq.com.py (reformas). One contextual link per page max.
- Schema: LocalBusiness + Service per page, FAQPage on FAQ blocks.
- Imagery: carpintero paraguayo en taller, cocina melamina terminada, pérgola con quincho de fondo.
